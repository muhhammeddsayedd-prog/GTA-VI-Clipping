First `score_parts`, each 1 to 10:

- `hook`: the first two seconds of your cut, for someone scrolling who knows nothing.
  - 9-10: opens on action or a line that raises a question at once ("why is he on fire?").
  - 7-8: opens on a clear, lively line that sets up what is coming.
  - 5-6: opens on ordinary talk; the interest starts a few seconds in.
  - 3-4: opens in the middle of a thought, or on filler or dead air.
  - 1-2: nothing in the opening would hold anyone.
- `clarity`: could a stranger say what happened after one viewing?
  - 9-10: obvious with the sound off and no context.
  - 7-8: clear from the lines alone; no backstory needed.
  - 5-6: followable, but who is who or why it matters takes guessing.
  - 3-4: needs knowledge of the stream, the server or earlier events.
  - 1-2: unintelligible or disjointed speech.
- `payoff`: the single moment the clip exists for.
  - 9-10: a surprise that lands hard: a sudden reversal, a spectacular fail or win, a line people will quote.
  - 7-8: a specific moment you can point to in the lines (a crash, a punchline, a catch, a reveal) and a reaction that shows it landed.
  - 5-6: something mildly funny or tense happens, but there is no clear peak, or the peak is told rather than shown.
  - 3-4: build-up with no payoff, or the payoff is outside the cut.
  - 1-2: nothing happens.
- `rewatch`: would someone watch it twice or send it to a friend?
  - 9-10: people will tag a friend or loop it.
  - 7-8: satisfying enough to watch again once.
  - 5-6: fine once, then forgotten.
  - 3-4: most viewers leave before the end.
  - 1-2: no reason to watch at all.

Then `score`, the overall 1 to 10, from these bands:

- 9-10, share-worthy: a complete little story with a payoff that surprises, clear to a stranger, with a quotable line or a spectacular moment. `payoff` and `clarity` are both 8 or more. Example: the streamer brags that he never crashes, a truck launches him off a bridge the next second, and his passenger asks "so about that?" in a flat voice.
- 7-8, solid: a clear setup and a payoff that lands, understandable with no backstory. `payoff` and `clarity` are both 7 or more. Example: a traffic stop where the officer slowly realises the car is full of stolen parking meters, ending on his "you have got to be kidding me".
- 5-6, forgettable: something happens, but the payoff is mild, or it leans on context, or it is mostly banter with one good line. Example: two players argue about who dented the car, it is amusing, and it trails off without a punchline.
- 3-4, weak: talk with no real moment, a payoff that is missing or outside the cut, or a scene only regular viewers would follow. Example: the streamer explains his plan for a heist and the clip ends before anything is tried.
- 1-2, nothing: dead air, reading chat, menus, unintelligible speech.

Rules that make the number repeatable:
- `score` is never more than one above `payoff`, and never more than two above `clarity`.
- A clip that is mostly talking with nothing happening, or has no payoff you can point to in the lines, scores 5 or less.
- Do not raise a score because the streamer is well known, the chat was loud or the spike detector was confident; score the clip a stranger would see.
