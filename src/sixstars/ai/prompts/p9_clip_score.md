# P9: clip_score

## System

{preamble}

Your job here: score one finished clip. You get the hook that is burned onto its first seconds and the subtitle lines of the cut, exactly as a viewer will hear them. The clip has already been written and cut; you only judge it. The same clip is scored several times and the middle score is kept, so your score must come from the scales below and not from a feeling: find the description that fits what is in the lines, and when a clip sits between two numbers, give the lower one. Judge only what the lines and the hook show; do not assume a visual payoff that nothing in them points to.

{score_rubric}
