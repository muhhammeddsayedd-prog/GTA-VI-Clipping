### Public API

```python
import sixstars.vod
sixstars.vod.register_handlers()        # vod.process, replay.scan, vod.cleanup -> services.worker (idempotent)
sixstars.vod.job_handlers()             # {job type: handler}

from sixstars.vod import process
process.process_video(video_id, *, deps=None, final_attempt=False, force=False) -> Outcome
process.handle(job, deps=None) -> Outcome          # what the two handlers call
process.give_up(video_id, error) -> "done" | "failed"

Outcome(status, reason, run_after, kind, source, messages, spikes, confirmed, downloaded, queued, timings_s)
# status: done | deferred | skipped | failed | ignored
# kind (deferred): download_blocked | ai_budget | transcribe_budget | paused | emergency_stop | concurrency
```

| Job type | Queue | Payload | Enqueued by |
|---|---|---|---|
| `vod.process` | normal | `{"video_id": <videos.id>, ...}` (other keys are ignored) | discovery (M09), the lane itself when it defers |
| `replay.scan` | normal | `{"video_id": ...}` or `{"live_session_id": <live_sessions.id>}` (the `videos` row is created from the session when missing) | live lane (M14) |
| `vod.cleanup` | normal | `{"video_id": <videos.id>}`, priority 0, dedupe `vod.cleanup:<video_id>` | the lane |
| `clip.render` | normal | `RenderInput.to_payload()` of `factory.commands.input_from_spike(spike_id, file, source_offset_s=<file start in the video>, lane="hourly", mode="normal")` plus the P1 verdict; priority from `vod.render_priority` (55 for S and A, 40 for B); dedupe `clip.render:<spike_id>` | the lane |

The worker registration must call `sixstars.vod.register_handlers()`, `sixstars.factory.pipeline.register_handlers()` and `sixstars.discovery.tiers.register_handlers()`.

**Events.** Entity `video`: `video.processing`, `video.done`, `video.failed`, `video.skipped`, `video.deferred`, `video.blocked`, `video.retry` (each a status change, with `data.status`), `video.fallback_picked`, `vod.download_blocked`. Entity `spike`: `spike.render_queued`, `spike.failed`, `spike.rejected` / `spike.confirmed` for transcript moments. Without entity: `vod.download_blocked_notified`. Service `vod`.

**CLI.**

```
sixstars vod process <url> [--render] [--whisper-model small] [--force] [--json]
sixstars vod scan-only <url> [--top 25] [--details]
```

`process` creates the creator and video rows when they are missing (video details from `yt-dlp --dump-single-json`), runs the video in this process and prints the outcome; exit code 4 when it was deferred. `--render` also runs the queued `clip.render` jobs of that video here, through the factory. `--force` skips the blocked-downloads and concurrency gates. `scan-only` downloads the chat and prints the spike table; it stores nothing.

### How a video moves

`new` → `processing` → `done`. A deferral puts it back to `new` with `skip_reason = "<kind>: <reason>"` and queues the same job again for `run_after` (dedupe key flips between `vod:<id>` and `vod:<id>:again`, because the deferred job is still running when its successor is queued). A deferral uses no attempt. A raised error puts the video back to `new` (`video.retry`) and the job queue retries it (30 s, 120 s, 600 s); after the last attempt `give_up` closes it: `done` when at least one render was queued, else `failed` with a `warn` notification. `skipped` when the creator's tier is `restricted` or `blocked`.

**Idempotency.** A duplicated job for a finished video is ignored. A second job for a video in work is ignored (the claim is one conditional `update`); a `processing` row not updated for `vod.processing_stale_s` can be taken over. A retried job reuses the chat file (`DATA_DIR/chat/<platform>/<id>.jsonl`), finds its spikes by position (`persist_spike`), asks P1 only about spikes still `detected`, runs the fallback once (event `video.fallback_picked`), reuses a complete window file, and queues a render only when no `clip.render` job was ever queued for the spike. Covered by integration tests (duplicate job, retry after a failed window, block then success).

**Files.** `DATA_DIR/vod/<video_id>/work/` (captions, audio) is removed when an attempt ends, whatever the result. `DATA_DIR/vod/<video_id>/sections/<spike_id>.mp4` must stay until its render ran, so it is removed by `vod.cleanup` (every `vod.cleanup_interval_s`, once the spike is `clipped`, `rejected` or `failed`, or after `vod.section_retention_s`). A failed video with no render leaves nothing on disk.

### Download-blocked state

`ytdlp.classify_failure` recognises the bot check by its text, with a plain or a typographic apostrophe: `Sign in to confirm you're not a bot`. Also treated as a block: HTTP 429, "This content isn't available, try again later", "The page needs to be reloaded". Tests use the exact text.

When a caption request or a window download is blocked:

1. Event `vod.download_blocked` (platform, detail, whether proxy and cookies are set).
2. The video goes back to `new` (`video.blocked`), its job is queued again `vod.blocked_backoff_s` (1800 s) later as a fresh job. It never becomes `failed`, however often this repeats. Confirmed spikes stay `confirmed`.
3. At most one `warn` notification per `vod.blocked_notify_interval_s` (3600 s), titled "Video downloads are blocked": the error text, how many videos wait, and the fix (set `PROXY_URL` or `YOUTUBE_COOKIES_FILE` in the server's `.env`, restart the workers).
4. For the next `vod.blocked_backoff_s` every other video of that platform is deferred at its first gate: no chat download, no AI call, no yt-dlp run. After that one video probes again.

The caption request comes before P1, so on a blocked server no AI money is spent on videos that cannot be downloaded. If every window of a video ends with HTTP 403 and none came through, that is treated as the same block. Once a proxy or cookies are added the queued jobs run through at their next retry (within 30 minutes): the stored spikes and verdicts are reused.

`PROXY_URL` is used for platforms in `downloads.proxy_platforms`; `YOUTUBE_COOKIES_FILE` only when the file exists.

**Not verified:** nothing was run from a Hetzner address, with a proxy or with cookies. The classification is tested against the quoted text, not against a live block.

### Verified facts and differences from the kit

1. **Windows are stream-copied, not re-encoded.** `docs/08` asks for `--force-keyframes-at-cuts`. Without it ffmpeg copies from the key frame before the start and writes an edit list; decoded with ffmpeg the file starts on the asked frame. Checked on YouTube (the frame at 10 s is identical in a copied and a re-encoded cut of the same window) and on Twitch (SSIM 0.992 at 5 s). The copy costs no CPU and gives a file a quarter of the size (57 MB against 227 MB for 90 s of 1080p60). `vod.section_exact_cuts = true` brings the re-encode back.
2. **YouTube serves a window at about 1.5 times playback speed** on this connection, copied or re-encoded, with or without a JavaScript runtime: 58 to 64 s for a 90 s window. So windows are downloaded `vod.download_workers` (3) at a time.
3. **Format choice**: H.264 with AAC first (`bv*[height<=H][vcodec^=avc1]+ba[ext=m4a]`), then any codec. One download of the AV1 format failed with HTTP 403 from the media server; H.264 did not.
4. **yt-dlp 2026.10.26 warns that YouTube extraction without a JavaScript runtime is deprecated** ("No supported JavaScript runtime could be found. Only deno is enabled by default"). Downloads work today without one. The server image should get `deno`.
5. **Caption tracks**: a 4-hour stream's track is 3 MB and arrives in 5 s, with a time per word. The automatic track in the language spoken is named `<code>-orig` (`en-orig`, `hi-orig`). A machine-translated track (`en` on a Hindi stream) is refused with HTTP 429 and ends the yt-dlp run, so the default `vod.caption_langs` is `[".*-orig", "en"]`: the spoken-language track is fetched first and used as it is (the prompts read Hindi). A failure that names "subtitles" counts as "no captions", not as a block.
6. **`chat.download_replay` crashes when `YOUTUBE_COOKIES_FILE` names a file that does not exist** (`FileNotFoundError` in `chat/youtube_innertube.new_client`). The kit's `.env` has such a path. The lane calls the chat adapters itself and passes the cookies file only when it exists (`process.download_chat`).
7. **Chat reaches the confirmation with what was said.** With `vod.confirm_with_captions` the words of the padded window are put into the P1 input. P1 then rejects polls, audio checks and greetings by name; it also gets more careful (see the review).
8. **Twitch**: window download through yt-dlp needs no key (live test). Chat through the real TwitchDownloaderCLI 1.56.5: 48,234 messages of VOD `2900689447`, spikes found (live test). The whole lane on a Twitch VOD is covered with the M03 fixture and fakes only.

### Deviations from the milestone file and docs/08

1. No full-video download anywhere, also not in the fallback (docs/08 step 9 downloads the video and cuts locally): captions or audio only, then the same window downloads as the chat path.
2. Copy instead of `--force-keyframes-at-cuts` (fact 1).
3. `control.emergency_stop` stops the lane from downloading and enqueuing renders (orchestrator brief); docs/16 says rendering continues.
4. Transcript moments are not sent to P1 (it judges chat). P3's confidence decides: `confirmed` from `vod.fallback_min_confidence` (0.5), tier A from `spikes.s_min_confidence`, else B.
5. The fallback also runs when a chat replay exists but gave fewer than `vod.fallback_below_spikes` (1) confirmed spikes.
6. "Retry once" for a missing YouTube chat replay is one more request after `vod.chat_retry_delay_s` (20 s) in the same job, not a later job.
7. `spike_floor` of a transcript moment is its middle, so the clip writer may move the start into the first half.
8. Worker wiring (build step 4) is not in this branch: `services/worker.py` belongs to another agent.
9. `tests/unit/test_smoke.py`: the `vod process` placeholder case was removed (the command is real now). One line outside my listed paths.
10. Constants that are not settings: yt-dlp socket timeout (30 s) and retries (3), the minimum share of a window that counts as complete (0.5), P1 input shape (5 top tokens, 1,500 characters of transcript), `vod process` metadata timeout (120 s).

### Starts and ends

Looked at in the nine rendered clips (stills at 0.3 s, quarter, half, three quarters, last second, end card: `C:\Users\iway\6stars-m08-out\stills\<clip id>.jpg`) and read in their transcripts.

- **The window is in the right place.** In the three chat clips the event is inside the window with room before it: the pickup hits 25 s after the window start (chat peaked 5 s after the hit), the joint is noticed 12 s after the window start (chat peaked 8 s later). The writer never needed the padding to reach the event; it trimmed the start inwards (by 16 s, 5 s and 0 s) and ended within 4 s of the window end. `spikes.lead_s = 20`, `spikes.tail_s = 5` and `spikes.context_pad_s = 15` stay as they are, and so does `live.chat_offset_s.youtube = 0`: replay chat on YouTube lags the event by 5 to 8 s, which the lead covers.
- **Ends:** 8 of 9 end on a finished line ("they are narks", "You're looking at 90 months", "I'm not resisting"). One ends in the middle of a sentence (gun store: "...with respect, you").
- **Starts:** 5 of 9 start cleanly; 4 start in the middle of a sentence ("the crime to get in a car accident", "the map to go", "gonna go cuffed", "line what is this line"). All four are windows where Whisper `small` wrote no punctuation at all, so the clip writer could not see where a sentence begins; the five clean ones have punctuation. So the cut depends on the transcript, not on the window; see the next point for what `medium` changes.
- **Re-render with `medium`** (the production setting; three of the four ragged clips, same windows): the transcripts are more accurate but still without punctuation. One start moved to the beginning of its sentence ("what is the ... this line"), one stayed in the middle of a sentence ("am I doing this to myself"), one moved to the start of the padding and the clip then ended in the middle of a sentence. The writer's scores for the same three windows were 7, 5 and 6 (before: 7, 6 and 7). So ragged starts are not a matter of `lead_s`, `tail_s` or the transcript model; they come from the clip writer cutting on subtitle lines when the transcript has no sentence marks. A rule in the factory that snaps the cut to the nearest pause between words (for example a gap of 0.4 s or more) would fix it; that is M06's code. `medium` took 69 to 130 s for 65 to 90 s of audio on this desktop, about three times `small`.

### Transcript fallback on real videos

### Bugs and gaps found elsewhere (not fixed, except where noted)

1. `chat.download_replay` / `chat.open_live` pass `YOUTUBE_COOKIES_FILE` on without checking that the file exists; `youtube_innertube.new_client` then raises `FileNotFoundError`. The kit's `.env` names `/secrets/youtube_cookies.txt`. On a server where that file is not mounted every YouTube chat call fails. Worked around in the lane; the live lane (M14) needs the same care or a fix in `chat/`.
2. **P2 fails with `InvalidModelOutput` ("not valid JSON: Extra data: line 3 column 1") now and then** (3 of 19 renders, all three in one run of six, each after the client's own repair retry). The reply seems to hold a second JSON value after the first. `clients/anthropic_client._parse` could take the first JSON value (`json.JSONDecoder().raw_decode`). M05 already asked for the reason to be logged.
3. P3 returned a 70-second moment twice in a row when given 10-second blocks (limit 60), which fails the whole `pick_moments` call although other parts were already paid for. The lane now calls it once per 30-minute part and leaves a failed part out.
4. Whisper `small` on Hindi speech: 3 to 4 minutes for a 90 s window and text the writer cannot use. `medium` was not tried on Hindi. Until non-English speech is known to work, discovery should prefer English streams; the two largest GTA 5 chats on YouTube are Hindi and M09 noted that their language is reported as English.
5. `gate.hourly` asks for a writer score of 7; seven of the nine clips rendered here scored 5 or 6, and the same window scored 7 in one run and 5 or 6 in the next. With that threshold the hourly lane will be short of clips; M07 should look at the score distribution before fixing it.
6. `factory.commands.input_from_spike` does not carry the P1 verdict; the lane adds it from `spikes.claude_reason`.
7. `docs/08` and `docs/16` (`control.emergency_stop`: "rendering continues") do not match this lane any more; not edited beyond the settings rows.
